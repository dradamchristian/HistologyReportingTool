Cancer resection examples

  • Gastric adenocarcinoma invading subserosa (pT3), 0/24 nodes positive, proximal and distal margins clear, radial margin clear, no lymphovascular invasion, no perineural invasion.
  • Colorectal adenocarcinoma extending through muscularis propria into pericolic fat (pT3), 4 of 20 nodes positive, extramural vascular invasion present, nearest margin 8 mm clear.
  • GIST, 6 cm spindle cell tumour, 2 mitoses, margins clear.
  • Colorectal liver metastases: CRLM 5mm
  • HCC 30mm 3mm margin, no nodes, background cirrhosis.
Cancer resection nodes accumulator
You can dictate nodes and pT stage in chunks (for example, slide by slide). The generator will total nodes and keep the worst pT stage.
  • "1/3 nodes, 4 nodes, 5/7 nodes, pT2 pT3"
  • Expected behaviour: nodes positive/examined = 6/14 and pT = pT3.
